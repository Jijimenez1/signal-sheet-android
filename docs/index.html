How to read the odds

Each number is the chance the stock finishes higher than today after the time you pick. It starts from history: a typical stock rises about 51% of the time over two weeks, 53% over a month, and 58% over a year. The sheet nudges that up or down by up to about 15 points.

60%+Strong
52–59Leans up
46–51Coin flip
Under 46Weak

What moves the number

Buzz: how much Reddit, YouTube and finance news are talking about it, and whether the talk is positive. Analysts: ratings, price targets and recent upgrades. Trend: where the price sits against its moving averages, its past month and its RSI, read from real daily prices pulled every Monday and blended with the week's research. Business: growth, profits and price compared with earnings.

Short holds lean on buzz and trend. Long holds lean on analysts and the business. When a company reports earnings inside your window, the odds get pulled toward 50%, because one report can swing it either way.

Use it wisely

A 62% stock still drops 38 times out of 100. Use the list to decide what to research, spread your money out, and only risk what you can afford to lose. This is not financial advice.
