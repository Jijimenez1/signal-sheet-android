How the odds work

Each number is the chance the stock finishes higher than today after the time you pick. It starts from history (a typical stock rises about 51% of the time over two weeks, 53% over a month, 58% over a year) and moves up or down by up to about 15 points.

60%+Strong
52–59Leans up
46–51Coin flip
Under 46Weak

What moves it: buzz on Reddit, YouTube and finance news; analyst ratings and targets; the price trend (moving averages, RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands); and the business itself (growth, profits, P/E). Short holds lean on buzz and trend, long holds on analysts and the business. Earnings inside the window pull the odds toward 50%.

A 62% stock still drops 38 times in 100. Use this to decide what to research, spread your money out, and only risk what you can afford to lose. Not financial advice.
